Our Beginnings

The Story of Baidik Puja

Baidik Puja Samagri Pasal (वैदिक पूजा सामग्री पसल) was founded in 2005, recognizing the difficulty retail shops faced in sourcing genuine, high-quality puja materials. What started as a local family endeavor has grown into one of Kathmandu’s most respected suppliers of spiritual items.

Today, managed by our family’s current generation, our business operates with the same core values: preserving the sanctity of spiritual tools and supporting the traditional artisans who make them. We partner with over 150 artisan families across Nepal and India, ensuring fair-trade wages and helping maintain ancient metal casting and clay crafting techniques.

From pure copper vessels to hand-cast heavy brass murtis, every single item in our warehouse goes through a strict verification process to ensure it complies with scriptural guidelines for worship. We take pride in being a trusted name for temples and retail shopkeepers.

Our Milestones

Timeline of Devotion

Over the years, our dedication to purity and authentic sourcing has driven our growth and expansion.

1995

The Foundation

Our family starts sourcing handcrafted clay diyas and copper puja plates directly from traditional potters and metal-casting artisans, establishing the firm's legacy.

2005

Expanding the Network

Established strong partnerships with Varanasi cooperatives for pure copper wares and holy Gangajal, and Vrindavan for premium Tulsi and Sandalwood malas.

2015

Bulk Supplies & Temple Orders

Expanded supply channels to serve larger temple trusts and spiritual retail shops across Nepal and neighbouring regions with custom wooden crate shipping.

2026

Digital Catalog Launch

Introducing a modern digital catalog to make it simple for spiritual shopkeepers to browse items and contact us directly via WhatsApp for bulk pricing.
