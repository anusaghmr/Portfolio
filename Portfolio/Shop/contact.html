Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for wholesale pricing?

As a dedicated wholesale supplier, our standard minimum order value is NPR 20,000 for domestic orders, and equivalent thresholds for bulk export orders. Smaller packing assortments are available for testing retail trials.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we coordinate international cargo shipments. Sourcing materials from Nepal and India, we export to distributors and temples in the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and South East Asia, assisting with customs invoice documentation.

How are fragile items (such as large brass murtis) packaged?

All fragile murtis and brass decorative items are securely packed in customized shock-absorbent bubble wraps, multi-layered corrugated boxes, and placed in wooden crates for cargo logistics to ensure zero damage in transit.

Can we request custom-sized statues or custom-labeled incense?

Absolutely. We collaborate directly with traditional metal-casting and wood-carving artisan guilds in Kathmandu and Jaipur. Custom deity idols and special corporate festival gifting assortments can be made to order. Please specify dimensions in your query.
